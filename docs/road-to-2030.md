# Research and contribution direction through 2030

The ambition is influential global security leadership, earned through reproducible work and independently evidenced value.

| Period | Focus | Evidence to earn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 foundation | Define the connected portfolio, scope, sector workspaces and local reference labs | Maintained sources, test records, credible attribution and a clear contribution route |
| 2027 implementation | Build landing-zone, isolated-operation, AI-authority and sector cases | Versioned implementations and authorized environment-specific results |
| 2028 evaluation | Seek peer review and independent reproduction | Reproduction records, accepted contributions or publications, and measured limitations |
| 2029 interoperability | Compare providers, suppliers, cryptographic transitions and recovery paths | Documented compatibility, benchmarks and repeatable exercises |
| 2030 impact | Demonstrate a maintained body of useful security work | Separately evidenced adoption, outcomes, research recognition and sustainable engagements |

These are goals. Acceptance, adoption, revenue and recognition need their own records.

## Daily contribution rule

Use the established daily contribution calendar. Add one bounded artifact: a source note, design decision, test, implementation change, result record, review response, or authorized disclosure lesson. Link it to a business process and the previous artifact. Publish work when its scope and evidence are clear.

## Building a sustainable practice

Use the public portfolio to help collaborators assess the work. Begin an engagement with a scoped decision, defined deliverables, accountable roles and acceptance criteria. Record attributable outcomes with the relevant permission. Build reusable methods and maintained tools from verified recurring needs.
